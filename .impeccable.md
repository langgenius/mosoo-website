# mosoo Website Design Context

- Users: developers and agent-platform teams evaluating, self-hosting, and operating mosoo.
- Goal: understand the product quickly, verify technical credibility, then open the console, docs, blog, or repository.
- Brand: calm, technical, open-source, precise, and quietly optimistic.
- Visual language: preserve the existing warm paper, forest ink, lime accent, framed layout, typography, spacing, and motion.
- Localization: English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese must carry the same product meaning while reading naturally in each language. Keep product and protocol terms such as Agent, App, runtime, API, CLI, SDK, MCP, BYOK, Threads, Runs, and sandbox unchanged.
- Accessibility: localize visible labels, image alternatives, titles, and ARIA labels; retain native controls and keyboard focus behavior.
- Routing: `/en`, `/zh`, and `/ja` are stable canonical pages, each with a `/pricing` subpage (`/en/pricing`, `/zh/pricing`, `/ja/pricing`). `/` and `/pricing` are only geo-aware entry points.
