# Human time cover brief

Core idea: the same human time can be felt and shared by an audience, or remain
hidden in production. Visibility shapes the audience's expectations. The ruler
is unnumbered: it is a metaphor, not a measured percentage of labour.

The author had already accepted the body Excalidraw illustration: one horizontal
ruler with its right half covered by a curtain. That composition is the primary
reference and remains unchanged in the article. The cover adapts it to the
existing Mosoo soft + fluid family: neutral paper, seeded hand strokes, a softly
folded curtain, a narrow `#84DE02` accent and ample space. It adds no extra
symbol or headline. Its labels are `Human time`, `Seen` and `Unseen`.

Motion makes visibility concrete. The curtain gently opens from a more concealed
position, stops at the half-open state, and leaves part of the work hidden.
The final static picture carries the idea on its own.
